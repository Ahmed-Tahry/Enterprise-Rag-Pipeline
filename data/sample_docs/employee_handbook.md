# Acme Corp — Employee Handbook 2024

## 1. Company Overview

Acme Corp was founded in 2010 with a mission to deliver world-class software solutions to enterprise clients. Headquartered in San Francisco, CA, with offices in New York, London, and Singapore, Acme Corp employs over 3,500 professionals globally.

Our core values are: **Customer First**, **Integrity**, **Innovation**, and **Inclusivity**. These values guide every decision we make — from product development to how we treat each other day-to-day.

## 2. Employment Policies

### 2.1 Working Hours

Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday, in the employee's local time zone. Flexible working arrangements are available with manager approval. Remote work is permitted for up to 3 days per week for roles that are eligible.

### 2.2 Leave Policy

**Annual Leave:** Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid annual leave per year, plus 10 public holidays. Leave must be requested at least 2 weeks in advance for periods longer than 3 consecutive days.

**Sick Leave:** Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. A medical certificate is required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days.

**Parental Leave:** Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Leave can begin up to 4 weeks before the expected birth date.

### 2.3 Performance Reviews

Performance reviews are conducted semi-annually — in June and December. Ratings are on a 5-point scale: Exceptional, Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, Below Expectations, and Unsatisfactory. Employees rated "Below Expectations" will receive a formal Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) within 30 days.

## 3. Benefits

### 3.1 Health Insurance

Acme Corp provides comprehensive health insurance through BlueCross BlueShield. Coverage includes medical, dental, and vision. Employees may add dependents at the open enrollment period each November. The company covers 80% of the premium for employees and 60% for dependents.

### 3.2 Retirement Plan

Employees are eligible to participate in the 401(k) plan after 90 days of employment. Acme Corp matches contributions up to 4% of the employee's base salary. Vesting is immediate for employee contributions; employer match vests over 3 years (33% per year).

### 3.3 Learning & Development

Each employee receives an annual Learning & Development budget of $2,000. This can be used for online courses, conferences, books, or certifications relevant to their role. Expenses require manager approval and receipts must be submitted within 30 days.

## 4. Code of Conduct

### 4.1 Workplace Respect

All employees are expected to treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect and professionalism. Harassment, discrimination, or bullying of any kind will not be tolerated and may result in immediate termination.

### 4.2 Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose any financial interest in, or employment with, a competitor, supplier, or customer of Acme Corp. Disclosure should be made to HR and the employee's direct manager. Undisclosed conflicts of interest are grounds for disciplinary action.

### 4.3 Confidentiality

Employees must keep all proprietary information, client data, and trade secrets confidential during and after employment. This obligation continues for 2 years post-employment for trade secrets and indefinitely for customer personal data.

## 5. IT & Security Policy

### 5.1 Acceptable Use

Company devices and network resources must be used primarily for business purposes. Limited personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with work responsibilities or violate any other policy. Installing unauthorised software on company devices is prohibited.

### 5.2 Data Classification

Data is classified into four tiers: **Public**, **Internal**, **Confidential**, and **Restricted**. Restricted data (including customer PII and financial data) must be encrypted at rest and in transit, and access must be logged. Only employees with a demonstrated business need may access Restricted data.

### 5.3 Password Policy

All passwords must be at least 12 characters and include uppercase, lowercase, number, and special character. Passwords must be changed every 90 days. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all systems that support it.

## 6. Expense & Reimbursement Policy

### 6.1 Travel

Business travel must be pre-approved by the employee's manager. Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours. Business class may be approved for flights over 6 hours with VP-level approval. Hotel stays should not exceed $250/night in standard markets and $350/night in high-cost markets (NYC, SF, London, Singapore).

### 6.2 Meals & Entertainment

Meal expenses up to $75/person for client entertainment are reimbursable with a receipt and business justification. Team meals are reimbursable up to $50/person and require manager approval. Alcohol expenses are reimbursable up to $25/person per event.

### 6.3 Submission Process

All expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense. Receipts are required for all items over $25. Reports are submitted via Concur Expense. Reimbursements are processed within 2 weeks of approval.

## 7. Disciplinary Process

Minor violations result in a verbal warning followed by a written warning. Repeated violations or serious misconduct may result in suspension without pay or termination. Gross misconduct (including theft, fraud, or violence) may result in immediate termination without notice. Employees may appeal disciplinary decisions to HR within 10 business days.

## 8. Offboarding

Employees leaving the company must return all company property (devices, access cards, parking passes) on their last day. Access to all systems will be revoked on the last day of employment. Departing employees must complete an exit interview with HR. Final pay, including accrued unused leave, will be processed in the final paycheck.
